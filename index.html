About HOM

Builders first. That's the difference you'll feel.

From garages and additions to major renovations and new homes, we bring the same standard to every job: a proper foundation, framing to code, real materials, and finishes that last. We build it right, not just fast.

That's also why so many owners come to us for a dwelling unit. Whether you're adding independent space for a parent, a suite for your adult children, or a rental that pays for itself over time, Brandon and the HOM crew handle the whole project, from the first site visit through to the final walkthrough.
