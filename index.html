Who We Are

A builder's builder — focused on the home that fits beside your home.

Most contractors treat an Additional Dwelling Unit as a bigger renovation. We treat it as what it is: a complete, code-compliant home, engineered and built to last. That distinction changes everything — the planning, the structure, the finish, and the outcome.

Whether you're creating independent space for aging parents, a private suite for your adult children, or a long-term income property, HOM Construction brings the discipline of custom home construction to every square foot. One team. One standard. No guesswork.
