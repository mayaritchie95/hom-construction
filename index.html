About HOM

A builder who treats an ADU like a home, not a bigger renovation.

An Additional Dwelling Unit is a full house in miniature. It needs a proper foundation, framing to code, real insulation, and finishes that last. That is how we build every one of them.

Whether you're adding independent space for a parent, a suite for your adult children, or a rental that pays for itself over time, Brandon and the HOM crew handle the whole job, from the first site visit through to the final walkthrough. You work with the same people the entire way.

  • ADUs, garden suites, and laneway homes
  • Permits, zoning, and trades managed for you
  • One point of contact from start to finish

Why Build an ADU

Why homeowners across the region are building ADUs.

An ADU is a serious investment, and the result comes down to who builds it. Here is what a well-built one does for you.

Rental Income

A finished suite can bring in steady monthly rent and add real value to your property for the long term.

Room for Family

Give a parent or an adult child their own space, with privacy on both sides, on your property or theirs.

Permits Handled

Setbacks, zoning, and permits are ours to manage. We take the project from first drawing to occupancy.

Built to Last

Proper foundation, framing, insulation, and finishes. A real home that holds up for decades, not a shed.
