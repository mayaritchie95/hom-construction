Accessibility Statement

Last updated:

HOM Construction Ltd. is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to all users, including people with disabilities, in keeping with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Our Commitment

  • Clear, readable typography and strong colour contrast;
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, links, and forms with visible focus states;
  • Descriptive labels for all form fields and interactive elements;
  • Responsive layouts that work across devices and screen sizes;
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We review our website regularly and work to improve the experience for every visitor.

Feedback

If you encounter any barrier using this website, or need information in an alternative format, please let us know through our contact form. We will do our best to accommodate your request.
